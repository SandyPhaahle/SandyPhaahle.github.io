01 / Profile

About

Professional infrastructure experience, postgraduate study and an emerging research direction.

I am a database and infrastructure professional with experience across Oracle and Microsoft SQL Server environments. My career has involved supporting complex enterprise systems, including business-critical financial-services infrastructure.

My professional experience has developed my understanding of system reliability, data integrity, recovery, access controls, operational risk and incident analysis. Working with critical systems has also shaped my interest in how technical systems behave under real-world constraints and how their risks can be identified and managed.

Alongside my engineering career, I am pursuing postgraduate computing research. I am interested in bringing an evidence-led, systems-oriented perspective to empirical research into the reliability, behaviour and safety of advanced AI systems.
